# Motion Sickness Assist

To help with motion sickness, listen to the following 100 Hz sound sample (with
headphones) for one minute before travelling.

The effect lasts for two hours after which you can listen to this again.

<p>
<App></App>

## How it works

It was discovered in a research that this specific frquency of sound simulates
the vestibular system (the sensory system in the inner ear that is responsible
for balance) and helps with dizziness caused by travelling in cars and buses.

::: info Reference
**Just 1-min exposure to a pure tone at 100 Hz with daily exposable sound
pressure levels may improve motion sickness** by Yishuo Gu, Nobutaka Ohgami,
